ARCADE — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ARCADE collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the bot on Discord.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, ARCADE may store:

* Discord user IDs.
* Discord server IDs.
* Virtual currency balances, points, levels, and achievements.
* Game-related information and gameplay progress.
* Bot configuration and settings for individual servers.

We do not intentionally collect passwords or Discord account login credentials.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

* Operate games and interactive features.
* Maintain player progress and virtual balances.
* Save server-specific settings.
* Prevent abuse, cheating, and duplicate rewards.
* Improve bot functionality and troubleshoot issues.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases or files used by ARCADE to maintain its functionality.

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required for legitimate operational purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable legal requirements, or protect the service from abuse.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users or server administrators may request the deletion of stored data associated with them or their server through our official support channels.

Requests will be reviewed and handled where applicable.

8. Children’s Privacy

ARCADE is not designed to collect personal information beyond what is reasonably necessary for its functionality. Users must comply with Discord’s age requirements and applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected in the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the ARCADE support team through our official Discord support channels.
